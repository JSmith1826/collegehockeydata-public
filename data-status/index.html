Source transition is explicit. Men's current shooting, goal-face maps, puck battles and chance comparisons use Instat. Canonical rosters, origins, ages, draft metadata and the longer historical series retain CHN/other reviewed sources. Assists, points, PIM and PP/PK retain their separately labeled CHN boxes, rebuilt from the current retained working database. Goalie SV%, GAA, appearances and workload use CHN game boxes reconciled to opponent shots and the final score. Instat goalie IDs and chart-derived save counts remain research evidence; roster membership alone does not validate who played. Women's data remain CHN/NCAA.
