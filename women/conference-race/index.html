05 / ABOUT THE DATA

What the lines mean.

Conference scoringLoading scoring rules...

Average paceA three-point game produces a league average of 1.5 points per team appearance. Pace shows the distance above or below that line.

Verified resultsLoading coverage...

The horizontal axis shows conference games played through the selected date. Teams with games in hand end farther left; the date slider controls which results are visible. The axes follow the visible results, with the full schedule length shown in the heading. Maximum possible points and the previous season's winning total are optional and off by default.

Ghosted logos mark teams whose remaining maximum is below the current leader. This is a points-based elimination test; conference tiebreakers and constraints between remaining matchups are not applied. Custom date markers show each team's position on that date; focus a program to see marker labels.
