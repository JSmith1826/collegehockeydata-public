05 / ABOUT THE DATA

Built from every recorded goal.

Game balanceTime is accumulated between goals in seven states, from leading by three or more to trailing by three or more.

Overtime clockSudden-death games stop at the winning goal. Scoreless overtime and shootout games run through 65:00.

Data coverageThe unified archive stores final scores but not goal timestamps. This view combines historical goal timelines and recovered CHN box scores. Games with incomplete scoring timelines are excluded.
