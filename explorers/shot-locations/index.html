About the shot locations

Geography, not expected goals

These areas use the reviewed shot-map boundaries on CHN's rounded display grid. They describe recorded origins; distances are not calibrated feet and the zones do not estimate scoring probability.

Accepted mapped games

Each team's locations require explicit direction and matching goals. A one-shot difference in on-net totals is accepted and flagged; source dots are retained. Each axis uses its own accepted mapped-game denominator, including zero-attempt games. Missing charts, unknown directions, goal disagreements and larger shot discrepancies are excluded for the affected side. Historical coverage can be incomplete and unrepresentative.

What is counted

Unblocked attempts = goals + saves + misses. On-net shots = goals + saves. All attempts also includes blocks. All periods and situations are combined; empty nets cannot be excluded. Matching on-net totals does not establish complete reporting of misses and blocks.

References and filters

Guides compare displayed team averages with equal weight. Conference focus preserves the national context; conference-only display narrows the field. Qualification uses accepted mapped games. Trend lines and sample SD describe differences across team averages.
