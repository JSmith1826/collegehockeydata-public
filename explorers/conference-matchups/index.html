ABOUT THIS VIEW

Final scores. Season-specific leagues.

Wins and losses follow the final score, including overtime. Equal final scores remain ties; shootout decisions are ignored. Winning percentage is wins divided by games, with ties in the denominator, matching the original notebook. Goals and margins are averaged over the selected matchup’s games.

Exhibitions and known lower-division opponents are excluded. All counted games includes NCAA postseason; the filter removes those games. Holiday tournaments remain included. Independents appear only when season-specific membership evidence identifies them explicitly.

Membership evidence and coverage

Unknown or conflicting membership is excluded rather than assigned to Independent. Historical results can be partial, so totals describe the archived games available here.
