Definitions, membership & coverage

Winning percentage: (wins + 0.5 × ties) / games. Each reciprocal pair adds to 100%. Overtime wins count as wins; equal final scores stay ties and shootouts are ignored. Win rate is a separate measure with no credit for ties.

Scoring: Goals scored / game uses the row's goals. Goal difference / game subtracts opponent goals. Range averages are calculated from all counted games, rather than averaging season percentages.

Membership: Every game uses its season's conference assignments. Ranges pool leagues by their historical name, even when their membership changed. Independents are a comparison group, not a conference; games between two independents are excluded. The contemporary CCHA mark is omitted in ranges that include its original era before 2013.

Scope: Counted games have a final score and known membership on both sides. Exhibitions, known lower-division opponents, and same-group games are excluded. Game scope can include, exclude, or isolate NCAA postseason games. Holiday tournaments count in the full and NCAA-excluded views. Source archives can be incomplete. Colors describe results and volume, not an adjusted ranking or schedule strength.
