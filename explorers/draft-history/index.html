04 / ABOUT THE DATA

Read the draft timeline correctly.

Entering the seasonA player’s draft year is the season’s starting calendar year or earlier. This is the draft footprint the roster carried into the fall.

Selected afterwardA later draft year is retrospective evidence. It can help identify development paths, but the player was not yet an NHL selection during the displayed season.

No selection recordedPlayers outside these bars are never labeled permanently undrafted. Current players may be selected later, and the 2003–04 and 2004–05 archived roster sources do not provide complete draft status.
