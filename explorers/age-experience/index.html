06 / ABOUT THE DATA

WHAT COUNTS AS EXPERIENCE.

AgeCalculated on October 1 of the selected roster season from the birth date reported by College Hockey News.

Academic classThe roster’s reported freshman, sophomore, junior, senior, or graduate designation.

Games playedDistinct recorded D1 games before the selected season, including games at other programs. Exhibitions and the selected season are excluded. ≥ marks incomplete earlier box coverage; — means unknown. Goalies need recorded playing time or saves, so an unused backup does not gain an appearance. Team averages use players with known counts.

Observed D1 roster historyThe number of earlier seasons in which the same player appears on a collected Division I roster. It is a roster-history measure, not an NCAA eligibility ruling.
