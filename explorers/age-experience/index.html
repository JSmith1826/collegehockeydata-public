06 / ABOUT THE DATA

WHAT COUNTS AS EXPERIENCE.

AgeCalculated on October 1 of the selected roster season from the birth date reported by College Hockey News.

Academic classThe roster’s reported freshman, sophomore, junior, senior, or graduate designation.

Observed D1 experienceThe number of earlier seasons in which the same player appears on a collected Division I roster. It is a roster-history measure, not an NCAA eligibility ruling.
