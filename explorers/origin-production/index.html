04 / ABOUT THE DATA

Definitions and coverage.

Previous leagueThe origin bin comes from each roster's reported last team and league. It is not birthplace or proof that a player transferred that year.

Verified movementReturning and verified transfer require the same player ID on adjacent-season rosters. “No prior membership observed” can include first-years and gaps in the archive.

CoverageA comparison is flagged when fewer than 75% of roster players have a same-season stat link, fewer than 80% of scored team games have player rows, or more than 25% have unknown origins. Individual bins under 100 recorded appearances for the selected measure are marked small sample.
