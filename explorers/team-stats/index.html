03 / ABOUT THE DATA

Data and calculations

One game, two sides

Only scored, non-exhibition matchups between teams with CHN rosters are included. Every team's rate uses its own games in the selected date range. Games against those opponents count even when you display just one conference.

Recorded metrics and coverage

Goals come from final scores. Shots, penalty minutes, and power-play figures require recorded totals for both teams. Expected goals uses CHN-reported xG, averaged over games with xG recorded for both teams; it does not require an available shot chart. Missing fields never become zero. Each chart reports how many games support its metric.

Qualifications and rates

The selected minimum applies to both scored and paired metric games. Power play is total goals / opportunities; penalty kill is one minus opponents' goals / opportunities. Zero opportunities produce an unknown rate. W/L/T uses the on-ice final score, not shootout standings decisions.

Reference guides and trends

Each plotted team contributes one value. The optional trend is an ordinary least-squares fit of the vertical metric on the horizontal metric. Standard-deviation guides show the mean and ±1 sample standard deviation across plotted team averages; they describe the spread between teams, not uncertainty for an individual team. Conference highlighting keeps the full-field reference population; conference-only mode recalculates it for that conference.

Source: College Hockey News schedules and archived/CHN box totals. This is an independent visualization by u/BakedMitten.
