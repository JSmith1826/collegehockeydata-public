03 / ABOUT THE DATA

Data and calculations

One game, two sides

Only scored, non-exhibition matchups between teams with CHN rosters are included. Every team's rate uses its own games in the selected date range. Games against those opponents count even when you display just one conference.

Metrics need box totals

Goals come from final scores. Shots, penalty minutes, and power-play figures require recorded totals for both teams. Missing box fields never become zero. Each chart reports how many games support its metric.

Special teams

Power play is goals divided by opportunities. Penalty kill is one minus opponents' power-play goals divided by opponents' opportunities. A team needs at least five scored games to appear, plus valid opportunities for both percentages.
