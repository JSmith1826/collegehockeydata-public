04 / ABOUT THE DATA

Definitions and coverage.

What hometown meansThe hometown reported on a College Hockey News season roster. It is not presented as a verified birthplace.

How ranges workPlayers count once for every selected season roster on which they appear. Production is summed from those selected player-seasons.

Production and ratesCounting statistics are divided by recorded player games and multiplied by 30. A statless current roster uses labeled cumulative prior NCAA totals; ranges omit that fallback to prevent double counting.

Map coverageOnly hometowns with reviewed coordinates appear on the map. Territory shading uses U.S. Census and Statistics Canada boundaries; Great Lakes masks use Natural Earth; per-capita views use 2020 U.S. and 2021 Canadian census populations.
