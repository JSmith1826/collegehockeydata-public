05 / ABOUT THE DATA

What the lines mean.

Common scoringRegulation wins are worth three points. Overtime and shootout wins are worth two; the losing team receives one.

Average paceA three-point game produces a league average of 1.5 points per team appearance. Pace shows the distance above or below that line.

Verified shootoutsEvery tied conference game in the covered seasons is matched to its CHN box score so the shootout winner receives two points and the losing team receives one.
