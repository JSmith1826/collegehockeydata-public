04 / ABOUT THE DATA

Definitions and coverage.

What “origin” meansThe previous team and league printed on a season roster. Transfers identify reported prior NCAA Division I experience at a different program; same-program records with no earlier pathway remain visibly unknown.

How draft context is usedNHL selections remain visible in player tooltips, team annotations, the drafted-share ranking, and program history. The dedicated Draft History Explorer handles deeper comparisons by round and selection timing.

How it stays currentThe page is exported from the canonical database’s derived team-construction CSV. Rebuild that CSV after a roster refresh, then regenerate the web data. Team logos come from this project’s supplied logo folder.
