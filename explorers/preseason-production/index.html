04 / READ THE NUMBERS

A record, not a prediction.

Tracked productionPoints, goals, assists, shots, penalty minutes, and player-game appearances come from the previous season’s scored non-exhibition player rows linked by CHN player ID. Transfers in are counted for their new roster; their former team records them as transfers out.

How exits are labeledAdjacent CHN rosters identify D1 transfers. A captured CHN Player Tracker row can verify professional and other outcomes. Without one, a senior or graduate roster class is shown as an inference; every other absence stays unknown.

What is coveredOpen the coverage note beside the filters for game-stat, identity, and Player Tracker coverage. Goaltenders are excluded. The chart does not adjust for playing time or predict new-season totals.
