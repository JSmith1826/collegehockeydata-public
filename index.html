Behind the project

Built for the curious fan.

College Hockey Data combines current College Hockey News records with archived season, roster, game, and draft material. It began as a series of visual posts for r/collegehockey and keeps growing from the questions people ask there.

This is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with the NCAA, College Hockey News, or any conference or school.
