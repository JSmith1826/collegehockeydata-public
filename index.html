02 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A record,
with its limits visible.

College Hockey Data combines current College Hockey News records with archived season, roster, and game material. The explorers keep missing and unclassified records visible so the charts do not claim more certainty than the source supports.

Early-season coverage is partial. Modern tools use the most reliable seasons available for each question and explain their coverage inside the page.
