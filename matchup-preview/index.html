Sources, definitions and coverage

Source: Hudl Instat ordinary goals, SOG and shot coordinates; College Hockey News rosters, assists, points and goalie boxes; draft observations checked October 16, 2026. Exhibitions are excluded. Scoring and records cover all completed games through the displayed cutoff; maps use only games whose locations pass the published verification checks.

Heatmaps show the share of each team’s mapped shots on goal, using one color scale for both teams. Rings mark goals. These are shot patterns, not a model of shot quality. Defensive maps show where opponents shot, viewed from the opponent’s attacking direction. Blocked attempts are the opponent’s recorded attempts marked blocked; they do not identify the blocker. Verification reconciles goals and shots on net; it does not independently establish complete misses or blocks. Recorded block rates and shares describe this source sample.

Class is the reported roster class, not age. Pathways describe the reported previous league, with transfers grouped separately. Players to watch combine draft pedigree, recorded experience and scoring; they are not projected lineups. Goalie rates use only reported appearances, exclude empty-net rows and do not predict starters or availability.

Missing data remain unavailable. A season-debut team may show explicitly labeled prior-season scorers. This page runs entirely in your browser. Downloading a preview does not publish it.
