How locations are flagged

Behind-line locations fall strictly behind the source rink’s goal line. Frequent locations have at least three shown attempts at the same recorded coordinate; up to five per category are marked on each map. Ties favor more mapped games, then coordinate order. These are descriptive flags, not statistical outliers. The source’s rounded coordinates can make different real locations look identical. Shares use all shown attempts, including origins beyond this half-rink. League-difference colors do not change these counting rules.

Coverage & Instat source definitions

One ordinary-event sample.

Current men's shot origins and net placements use the same accepted Instat fixtures and ordinary events. Shootouts and the reviewed overturned goal are excluded. Goals reconcile to source period totals; SOG reconcile independently to player overview totals. The data-status page lists every program and any missing scopes.

Instat zones 41–49.

These are the provider's NHL shot-origin areas. Source zone codes are authoritative; we do not classify rounded coordinates into our former CHN danger zones. The drawing uses the provider's zone layout with a documented transform. Unrecognized zone codes remain unknown in totals. This geography is not a shot-danger model.

Coordinates and samples.

Ice positions use the Instat 60.96 × 25.91 m template, attacking toward +x; displayed lateral orientation follows the provider. Net values retain the source display transform. Numeric detail is not proof of tagging accuracy. A missing coordinate stays missing. Heat smoothing illustrates concentration, not scoring probability.

Shares include the selected outcome beyond the half-rink crop. Per-game rates use accepted mapped team appearances. Left/right always refer to the displayed attacking view. Every control, area inspection and export uses this same zone model.
